# ⚔️ Hex Infinity RPG — Help & FAQ

---

## 🌟 Welcome to Hex Infinity

Hex Infinity is a **DnD-flavored auto-battle RPG** with deep character progression, random loot, and an ever-expanding world. Think old-school MUD meets tactical hex combat — all in your browser.

Evil presses itself upon the land. Your quest: grow powerful, find legendary gear, and push back the darkness.

---

## 🧙 Getting Started

### Creating Your Character

From the **Character Select** screen tap **Create New Character** to begin. You will choose your race, class, name, and appearance — then assign starting stat points.

**Races** each provide unique stat bonuses and passive traits:
- Humans are well-rounded with no weaknesses
- Elves favor magic and speed
- Dwarves are hardy and resistant
- Half-Orcs hit harder and rage in combat

**Classes** define your combat role and skill trees:
- **Warrior** — Heavy melee, high survivability, battlefield control
- **Mage** — Elemental damage, area attacks, glass cannon
- **Rogue** — Fast strikes, critical hits, evasion
- **Cleric** — Healing, buffs, balanced offense

**Stats** determine your raw power — assign these at character creation and every level up:
- **STR** — Physical damage dealt and physical damage absorbed
- **DEX** — Dodge chance and critical strike chance
- **INT** — Magic damage and mana pool size
- **STA** — Health pool and survivability

> **Tip:** Don't stress about a perfect build. Any stat combination is viable. You earn more points every level.

## 🗺️ Navigation

### The Main Nav Bar

Five buttons run along the bottom of every screen:

| Button | Opens |
|--------|-------|
| 📊 Stats | Character stats, equipment, and stat allocation |
| 🎒 Items | Your inventory and equipped gear |
| ⚔️ Skills | Skill tree — learn and manage your skills |
| 🗺️ Map | World map — travel between locations |
| ☰ Menu | Save, Help, Release Notes, and Logout |

**Gold dot badges** appear on nav buttons when action is available — unspent stat points, unspent skill points, or new unreviewed loot.

### Town vs. Battle Map

**Town (Kulsen's Landing)** is your home base. Here you can:
- Talk to NPCs for services: buy gear, upgrade items, heal, deposit to the bank
- Allocate stat and skill points
- Manage your inventory and equipment
- Travel to battle maps via the World Map

**Battle Maps** are hex grid combat zones. Here you can:
- Move by tapping hexes
- Fight enemies with Auto-Battle enabled
- Loot fallen enemies
- Use skills from the skill bar
- Return to town via Menu → Return to Town

### Talking to NPCs in Town

While in town tap any NPC name in the **text feed** to begin dialogue. The NPC's available services appear as tappable response options at the bottom of the screen.

## ⚔️ Combat

### Auto-Battle

When you enter a battle map enemies will spawn and seek you out. To engage:

1. Move onto a hex near enemies to begin combat
2. Open **☰ Menu** and toggle **Auto Battle ON** — your character now fights, uses skills, and loots automatically
3. Auto-battle runs for up to **4 hours** before the timer expires

You can also fight manually by staying near enemies without toggling Auto-Battle.

### Skills

Active skills appear in the **skill bar** at the bottom of the battle screen. When Auto-Battle is on they trigger automatically based on cooldowns. You can also tap them manually.

Passive skills are always active — no action needed.

Up to **5 active skills** can be slotted in your skill bar at once. Manage your loadout in the ⚔️ Skills panel while in town.

### Death

If your character falls in battle:
- You are **resurrected at the Temple** in town automatically
- You may **lose some equipped items** depending on your death penalty
- Your health is fully restored

> **Tip:** The Priestess at the Temple can bless your character before battle to reduce death penalties.

## 💾 Saving Your Progress

Your game is saved automatically when you:
- Return to town from a battle map
- Purchase or sell items
- Equip or upgrade gear
- Allocate stat or skill points

To manually save at any time open **☰ Menu → Save Game**.

> **Important:** Progress is stored in your browser's local storage. Clearing browser data will erase your saves. Use the **Register** option to create a permanent account and protect your characters.

## 📈 Progression

### Leveling Up

Defeating enemies earns **XP**. Leveling up grants:
- **Stat Points** — Assign freely to STR, DEX, INT, or STA
- **Skill Points** — Spend in your class skill trees
- Increased base health and mana

Check your XP progress in the **📊 Stats** panel.

### Stat Allocation

Open **📊 Stats** and tap **Allocate** to spend unspent stat points. Stat allocation is only available **while in town**.

### Skill Trees

Each class has **3 skill branches** with 12 skills each. Open **⚔️ Skills** to view your trees. Skills unlock top-to-bottom — each requires the prior skill to be learned.

- **Active skills** — Triggered in combat, shown on the skill bar
- **Passive skills** — Always-on bonuses (damage, defense, crit, lifesteal, etc.)

### Gear & Equipment

Items drop from enemies and can be bought at shops. Open **🎒 Items** to equip gear. Hovering or tapping an item shows a stat comparison to your currently equipped piece.

**Item Rarities** (weakest to strongest):
- ⬜ Common → 🟩 Uncommon → 🟦 Rare → 🟪 Epic → 🟧 Legendary

Higher rarity means better base stats and greater upgrade potential.

### Upgrading Gear

Visit the **Blacksmith** (warriors & rogues) or **Enchanter** (mages & clerics) in town to upgrade your gear. Upgrades require **gold** and **crafting materials** dropped by enemies.

Upgrading improves an item's power tier and stats. Even a Common item upgraded to max tier can remain competitive.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How do I get better gear?**
Fight enemies on higher-level maps, buy from the shop, or upgrade your existing gear at the Blacksmith or Enchanter. Rare materials dropped by stronger enemies unlock more powerful upgrade tiers.

**Q: Can I reallocate my stat points?**
A respec system is planned for a future update. In the meantime choose thoughtfully — though there is no wrong build.

**Q: Why can't I allocate stats on the battle map?**
Stat and skill allocation is only available in town. Return to Kulsen's Landing first.

**Q: My auto-battle stopped — why?**
Your 4-hour timer may have expired, your character may have died, or you manually toggled it off. The Barracks housing upgrade extends this timer.

**Q: How do I travel to a new map?**
Open **🗺️ Map** and tap a location. Maps must be accessed through connected routes — you cannot skip ahead to distant areas.

**Q: What happens if I close the browser mid-combat?**
Your last save is preserved. Unsaved progress (gold and loot earned since last save) may be lost. The game simulates some offline progress when you return.

**Q: How do I unlock new skills?**
Earn Skill Points by leveling up, then open **⚔️ Skills** and tap any available skill in your tree to learn it.

**Q: Can I have multiple characters?**
Yes — up to 3 characters per registered account, or 1 character on a guest account. Switch between them from the Character Select screen.

**Q: What are crafting materials for?**
Materials drop from enemies and are used to upgrade gear at the Blacksmith or Enchanter. Higher-tier materials are required for more powerful upgrades. Materials are also used to upgrade your Housing structures.
